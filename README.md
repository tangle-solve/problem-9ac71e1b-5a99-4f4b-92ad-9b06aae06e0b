### CLIM-000008: Attribution of the recent planetary-albedo decline
- **Subfield:** Radiative forcing, clouds, and climate sensitivity
- **Open:** Separate aerosol changes, internal ocean variability, and temperature-driven cloud feedback in declining reflected sunlight. Close attribution against spectral radiation, cloud properties, ocean-temperature patterns, and heat uptake.
- **Status:** Goessling and colleagues linked the 2023 temperature surge to exceptionally low albedo, especially reduced low-cloud cover; the cloud-change causes remained unresolved.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Distinguishes persistent feedback and forcing changes from transient variability.
- **Key works:** Goessling, Rackow and Jung (2025; online 2024), [“Recent global temperature surge intensified by record-low planetary albedo”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adq7280).
- **Related:** Attribution with correlated drivers; radiometric calibration drift.
